# Story queue

One title per line. The scheduled draft takes the first one that has
not been made yet, writes it, and renders it.

A line starting with `#` is a comment. A title is "done" when an
episode exists whose title matches it, so nothing is made twice.

Add titles freely — this is the only thing the channel needs from you
to keep running. Running out of titles is the one way it stops.
