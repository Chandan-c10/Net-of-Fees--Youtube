# The Two Friends and the Bear

[SCENE 1]

IMAGE: two young travellers walking together along a path through a thick green forest, storybook illustration, wide shot

Once upon a time, two friends were walking through a thick forest.

[SCENE 2]

IMAGE: the two friends talking as they walk, hands clasped in a promise, sunlit forest path, storybook illustration, medium shot

They had promised to help each other whenever they were in danger.

[SCENE 3]

IMAGE: the forest path ahead growing darker, tall trees crowding in, storybook illustration, wide shot

The forest grew thicker as they walked. The path grew narrow.

[SCENE 4]

IMAGE: one single large brown bear, alone, stepping out of the trees onto the path ahead of them, storybook illustration, wide shot

Suddenly, they saw a big bear coming towards them.

[SCENE 5]

IMAGE: both men frozen in fear on the forest path, the bear approaching, storybook illustration, medium shot

Both friends were terrified.

[SCENE 6]

IMAGE: one man scrambling quickly up the trunk of a tall tree, storybook illustration, wide shot

One friend quickly climbed up a tree to save himself.

[SCENE 7]

IMAGE: the man safe in the branches looking down, the other man alone below, storybook illustration, wide shot

He did not stop to help his friend. He did not even look back.

[SCENE 8]

IMAGE: the second man standing alone on the path, frightened, the bear coming closer, storybook illustration, medium shot

But the other friend did not know how to climb trees. He was very frightened.

[SCENE 9]

IMAGE: close-up of the second man's face, frightened but thinking, storybook illustration

Then he remembered something. He had heard that bears do not touch the dead.

[SCENE 10]

IMAGE: the man lying flat and completely still on the forest floor, eyes closed, storybook illustration, medium shot

So he lay down on the ground and held his breath.

[SCENE 11]

IMAGE: the large bear standing over the motionless man on the ground, storybook illustration, wide shot

The bear came near him.

[SCENE 12]

IMAGE: one single bear, alone, lowering its head to sniff at the man's face and ear, storybook illustration, close-up

It sniffed his face. It sniffed his ears.

[SCENE 13]

IMAGE: one single bear, alone, turning away, the man still lying motionless, storybook illustration, medium shot

The bear thought he was dead.

[SCENE 14]

IMAGE: one single bear, alone, walking away into the trees, the forest quiet again, storybook illustration, wide shot

After some time, it went away.

[SCENE 15]

IMAGE: the first man climbing down from the tree, the second man sitting up on the path, storybook illustration, medium shot

When the danger was over, the friend who had climbed the tree came down.

[SCENE 16]

IMAGE: the first man speaking curiously to his friend on the forest path, storybook illustration, medium shot

He asked, "What did the bear whisper in your ear?"

[SCENE 17]

IMAGE: close-up of the second man's face, calm and serious, looking directly at his friend, storybook illustration

The other friend replied, "The bear told me never to trust a friend who leaves me alone in danger."

[SCENE 18]

IMAGE: the first man looking down at the ground, ashamed, unable to meet his friend's eyes, storybook illustration, medium shot

The first friend felt ashamed of what he had done.

[SCENE 19]

IMAGE: one figure walking alone away down the forest path into the evening light, storybook illustration, wide shot

The moral of the story. A true friend is the one who stands by you in difficult times.
