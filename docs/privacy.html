Privacy policy

Net of Fees · last updated 10 November 2026

Who this covers

Net of Fees is a personal tool, run by one person on their own machine, to upload videos to their own YouTube channel. It has no other users. There is nothing to sign up for and no account to create.

What it accesses

With the owner’s explicit consent through Google’s sign-in screen, the tool is granted permission to upload videos to that person’s own YouTube channel. It requests the narrowest permission that allows an upload. It does not read a user’s subscriptions, watch history, comments, contacts, email, files or any other Google data.

What it stores

A Google authorisation token, so the owner does not have to sign in for every upload. The token is stored on the owner’s own computer, or in private storage that only the owner can read. It is never shared, never transmitted to any third party, and never placed in the public source repository.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising of any kind.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.
  • No cookies are set; this website has no server-side component.

Deleting the data

The owner can revoke the tool’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token. Deleting the token file removes it entirely.

Google’s terms

Use of the YouTube Data API is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the Google consent screen for this application.
